Effective date: October 19, 2026 · Last updated: October 19, 2026

MiniTV is a short-drama streaming service. This policy explains what we collect when you use minitv.us, h5.minitv.us and the MiniTV mobile apps (together, the “Service”), why we collect it, who we share it with, and the choices you have. We do not sell your personal information and we do not run advertising trackers.

1. Who we are

MiniTV (“we”, “us”) operates the Service. For the purposes of the EU/UK General Data Protection Regulation we act as the data controller for the personal information described below. You can reach us at xuliang2022@gmail.com.

2. Information we collect

2.1 Account information

  • Email sign-in. When you sign in with an email address we store that address and send you a one-time verification code. Codes expire after 10 minutes and are deleted after use.
  • Google sign-in. If you choose “Continue with Google”, Google shares your Google account identifier, verified email address, display name and profile picture with us. We use them to create or link your MiniTV account. We never receive your Google password.
  • Profile. You may edit your display name, avatar and bio inside the app.

2.2 Activity information

  • Dramas and episodes you open, playback position and whether you finished an episode, so you can continue watching on any device.
  • Likes, favorites and saved lists.
  • Coin balance, check-in streaks and reward tasks you complete.

2.3 Device and technical information

  • A randomly generated device identifier stored in your browser or app storage, used to keep sessions and anonymous activity consistent. It is not an advertising ID.
  • Standard server information such as IP address, user agent, requested URL, response status and timestamp, kept for security, abuse prevention and troubleshooting.

2.4 What we do not collect

We do not collect payment card details in the web player (subscriptions and coin purchases are handled by the app stores), we do not build advertising profiles, and we do not use third-party advertising or cross-site tracking cookies.

3. How we use information

  • Provide the Service — authenticate you, stream video, remember your progress, lists and preferences.
  • Operate rewards — track check-ins, tasks and coin balances so unlocks are credited correctly.
  • Security — detect abuse, fraud, credential stuffing and abnormal traffic, and keep our systems reliable.
  • Service messages — deliver sign-in verification codes and important account notices. We do not send marketing email without your consent.
  • Legal compliance — meet applicable legal obligations and respond to lawful requests.

We rely on contract performance to provide the Service, on legitimate interests for security and service improvement, and on consent where the law requires it.

California residents may request disclosure of the categories of information we collect and the business purposes for which we use it; we do not sell or share personal information as those terms are defined by the CCPA/CPRA.

9. Children

The Service is not directed to children under 13 (or under 16 where required by local law), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

We use HTTPS in transit, hashed sessions, short-lived verification codes, access controls on our servers and least-privilege access for staff. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we work to protect your information and to limit what we keep.
